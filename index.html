Mintzy: Autonomous Stock Market Co-pilot

Organization: Gravity Research Pvt. Ltd. Tech: LangChain ReAct, FastAPI, Pinecone, Pydantic, Ollama

The Industry Challenge

Financial retail traders often drown in disparate data—news feeds, earnings statements, and technical indicators. Existing chatbots produce generic hallucinations because they lack bounded tool execution, risk profile enforcement, and session-isolated memory.

System Architecture & LangChain ReAct Engine

Harsh designed and deployed a multi-stage LangChain ReAct Agent workflow that operates on deterministic thought-action cycles. Instead of raw text output, every turn is mediated by Pydantic models:

  • ClarifyAction: If user risk parameters (e.g., HighRisk/HighReward, Balanced, LowRisk/FairReturn) are absent, the agent holds execution to request specific user inputs.
  • PredictAction: Invokes proprietary time-series models (RSI, Bollinger Bands, Ichimoku clouds, Comprehensive Risk-Return Ratio) over target ticker baskets.
  • ConsultAction: Triggers web search across real-time financial market sources.
  • FinishAction: Synthesizes analytical findings into clear, actionable investor briefings.

Vector Storage & Privacy Isolation

Document uploads (quarterly PDF reports, corporate filings) are chunked via LangChain document splitters and indexed into Pinecone VectorDB. To ensure complete tenant data segregation across 100+ active users, queries are strictly partitioned using dynamic user_id namespaces and session_id metadata filters.

AadhaarOCR: 4-Stage Sub-500ms Extraction

Company: Monday Labs, LLC Tech: OpenCV, Perspective Warping, PyTesseract, Python

The Industry Challenge

Identity verification systems on mobile devices frequently encounter tilted, blurred, or high-resolution photos (12MP+). Feeding multi-megabyte raw photos directly into OCR causes sluggish latency (>5s) and frequent false reads due to surrounding background clutter.

The 4-Stage Pipeline

  1. Card Blob Detection & Isolation: Employs Canny edge contours and convex hull heuristics to detect the 4 card vertices, then applies an OpenCV getPerspectiveTransform / warpPerspective to flatten the card into an orthogonal view.
  2. Geometric Standardization: Downsamples the perspective-corrected card to a fixed 600×400 geometry. This reduces raw pixel count by over 95% while preserving crisp character edges.
  3. Noise & Non-Text Suppression: Automatically masks out the cardholder's photograph, holographic QR codes, Government emblems, and header banners.
  4. Information Segmentation: Executes PyTesseract on isolated ROIs and applies regex layout parsing to extract Name, DOB, Gender, and UID (12-digit Aadhaar Number) across both legacy and modern card revisions.

Performance Benchmarks

<500ms End-to-End Processing Time
>95% Pixel Volume Slashed
100% Field Extraction Reliability
